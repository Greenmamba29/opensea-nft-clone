# BNY Top 50 — Storefront Program Roster

**Source:** brooklynnavyyard.org tenant directory, scraped 2026-06-11 (314 tenants parsed into `research/parsed/part-a-e.json`, `part-f-o.json`, `part-p-z.json`).
**Machine-readable version:** `research/bny-top50.json`. **Program plan:** `PLAN-50-STORES.md` (repo root).

This is the ranked roster for the "50 storefronts" program: the 50 Brooklyn Navy Yard tenants best positioned to receive a pre-provisioned GrahmOS storefront. Ranking method, in order: (a) consumer-ready businesses with strong signals (press, scale, awards, anchor language), (b) clear website plus e-commerce evidence (`ecommerceHint: store`), (c) aisle coverage — every one of the 8 aisles gets at least 2 entries so Directions Mode can route through the whole mall. Cohorts: **1** = ranks 1–10 (pilot, press-proven consumer brands), **2** = ranks 11–30, **3** = ranks 31–50. All facts below come from the directory data — nothing is enriched or invented.

## Cohort 1 — first ten (why each)

1. **Russ & Daughters** — a 112-year-old New York institution that already anchors Building 77 with retail and a nationwide shipping hub; the single most credible anchor tenant the mall can show.
2. **Atoms** — Vogue-endorsed DTC sneaker brand with a live online store; instantly legible to consumers.
3. **Catbird** — press-proven Brooklyn jewelry brand physically expanding inside the Yard, with established e-commerce.
4. **Stick With Me Sweets** — Oprah's Favorite Things, Michelin Guide, NYT; luxury confections that ship, with an online store.
5. **Kings County Distillery** — NYC's oldest operating whiskey distillery with national press and competition awards.
6. **Aurate** — established DTC fine jewelry brand; storefront translates one-to-one to the mall model.
7. **ECCO Shoes** — a global premium footwear brand with a Yard presence; brand-name gravity for the directory.
8. **Care/of** — national DTC supplement brand manufacturing at the Yard; large existing online catalog.
9. **Brooklyn Roasting Company** — certified, locally roasted coffee with an online store; the literal "Yard Roast" story.
10. **Té Company** — NYT/New Yorker-featured tea company retailing loose-leaf teas and teaware online.

## Data gaps (read before outreach)

- **Empty descriptions (7 of 314):** ADL Distributors, Alsop Studios, Barden, UMR Channels Group (Build Up Purpose), J. Clyde Studio, Kintra Fibers, Whitney Wood Bailey. None made the 50, but Kintra Fibers has a `store` hint worth a second look.
- **Empty websites (46 of 314):** these were effectively disqualified regardless of consumer signals. Notable consumer-ready casualties: **Atsuyo Et Akiko** ("household name for a decade"), **Corridor NYC** (two stores, 120+ retail partners), **Underground Signs NYC**, **Lebogner**. If websites are found in enrichment, they are strong cohort-3 substitutes.
- **Thin aisles:** the directory itself only yields 2 plausible packaging tenants and very few consumer-facing logistics/office-services/fabrication tenants — those aisles are filled with strong B2B operators (`consumerReady: false`), which suit the quote/RFQ flow more than retail checkout.
- **Signal quality:** revenue and social-sentiment signals here are directory- and tenant-page-based only. There is no third-party revenue, ratings, or press verification in this dataset. **Enrich with press/ratings research before any outreach** — cohort assignments may shift.
- **`ecommerceHint` is a hint, not a fact:** 29 of the 50 show `store`; the remaining 21 are `unknown` and need a manual storefront check during provisioning to decide clone-vs-integration-invite (see `PLAN-50-STORES.md`).
