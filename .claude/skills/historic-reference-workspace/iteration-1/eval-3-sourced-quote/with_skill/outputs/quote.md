# Malcolm X quote for the GrahmOS mall landing page

## The quote

> "The economic philosophy of Black nationalism only means that we should own and operate and control the economy of our community."

A longer cut from the same passage, if the layout allows:

> "And the best place to start is right in the community where we live. ... Once you and I go into business, we own and operate at least the businesses in our community. What we will be doing is developing a situation wherein we will actually be able to create employment for the people in the community."

## How to credit it

**On the landing page (short form):**

> — Malcolm X, "The Ballot or the Bullet," Cleveland, April 3, 1964

**Full citation (footer, about page, or governance docs):**

Malcolm X, "The Ballot or the Bullet," speech delivered at Cory Methodist Church, Cleveland, Ohio, April 3, 1964. Transcript published in *Malcolm X Speaks* (George Breitman, ed., 1965).

## Why this quote fits GrahmOS

It is the documented source of the governance principle "economic self-determination of the community" in the GrahmOS principles board, and it speaks directly to the mall thesis: community-owned businesses where the community spends its money, so wealth and jobs recirculate within it.
