# Malcolm X quote for the GrahmOS landing page (baseline run)

**Recommended quote (landing-page ready):**
> "The economic philosophy of Black Nationalism... only means that we should own and operate and control the economy of our community."
> — Malcolm X, "The Ballot or the Bullet" (April 3, 1964)

**Extended version:**
> "We have to learn how to own and operate the businesses of our community and develop them into some type of industry that will enable us to create employment for the people of our community."
> — Malcolm X, "The Ballot or the Bullet," Cory Methodist Church, Cleveland, Ohio, April 3, 1964

**Alternative (community-dollar theme, fits a mall well):**
> "Our people have to be made to see that any time you take your dollar out of your community and spend it in a community where you don't live, the community where you live will get poorer and poorer, and the community where you spend your money will get richer and richer."
> — Malcolm X, "The Ballot or the Bullet" (April 3, 1964)

**Crediting:** minimal credit line for a hero section: `— Malcolm X, "The Ballot or the Bullet," 1964`; fuller credit for a footer/about page: `Malcolm X, "The Ballot or the Bullet," speech delivered at Cory Methodist Church, Cleveland, Ohio, April 3, 1964. Published in "Malcolm X Speaks" (Grove Press, 1965, ed. George Breitman).`

**Caveats (from the run):** wording varies slightly between the Cleveland (April 3) and Detroit (April 12) deliveries, so verify exact phrasing against *Malcolm X Speaks* before publishing; commercial use on a branded landing page may warrant a permissions check with the estate.
